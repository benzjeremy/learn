learn is the free, ad-free, and privacy-first open-source alternative to Mimo and SoloLearn.

Master modern programming languages like Go (Golang), Astro, Python, HTML5/CSS3, JavaScript, and PHP with these key advantages:

• 100% Non-Linear: Jump freely into any chapter — no forced lock-in on basic beginner exercises.
• No Paywalls & No Heart Barriers: Learn at your own pace without artificial wait times or monetization tricks.
• 100% Offline-First: All courses run completely offline in flight mode or on the subway.
• Zero-Telemetry & GDPR-Compliant: No ad trackers, no analytics SDKs, no Google Firebase dependency.
• Local Reminder System: Configurable daily habit reminders and gentle inactivity nudges without cloud push servers.
• Floating AI Robot Tutor: Docked at the screen edge for future interactive code explanations.
