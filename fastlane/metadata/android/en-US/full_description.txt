learn is the free, ad-free, and privacy-first open-source alternative to Mimo and SoloLearn.

Master modern programming languages like Go (Golang), Astro, Python, HTML5/CSS3, JavaScript, SQL, C#, and PHP with these key advantages:

• Real Code Empowerment: Didactic step-by-step lessons taking learners from basics to writing real-world software.
• Interactive Compiler Lab: In-browser bugfixing challenges with realistic compiler output, line numbers, and terminal exit codes.
• Option-Specific Didactic Feedback: No solution-spoiling on incorrect answers — the system explains why your chosen option is incorrect.
• 90-Minute Final Exams: Authentic exam simulations with countdown timer, grading scale (1–6), and reference solutions.
• No Paywalls & No Heart Barriers: Learn at your own pace without artificial wait times or monetization tricks.
• 100% Offline-First: All courses run completely offline in flight mode or on the subway.
• Zero-Telemetry & GDPR-Compliant: No ad trackers, no analytics SDKs, no Google Firebase dependency.
• Native In-App Legal & Privacy Notice: Complete offline Impressum (§ 5 DDG), Privacy Policy (GDPR/TDDDG), and contact information directly in the application.
• Local Reminder System: Configurable daily habit reminders and gentle inactivity nudges without cloud push servers.
• Floating AI Robot Tutor: Docked at the screen edge for future interactive code explanations.
